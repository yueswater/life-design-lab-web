# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: July 2026**\
**Effective date: July 2026**

Life Design Lab respects your privacy and your right to control your own personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is collected, processed, used, and protected when you browse this website and use its online booking feature, and identifies the third-party services that may be involved.

By using this website, you confirm that you have read and understood this Policy.

## Chapter 1 · General Provisions

### § 1 Scope

This Policy applies to Life Design Lab's official website and its online booking feature.

This Policy does not apply to:

1. Websites, social platforms, or third-party services not controlled or operated by Life Design Lab;
2. External websites you reach through links on this website;
3. The data collection and processing carried out by Supabase, our email delivery provider, or other technical service providers that help operate this website, on their own platforms — those are governed by each provider's own privacy policy.

### § 2 Personal Data We Collect

#### 2.1 What the Booking Form Collects

This website primarily collects personal data through its **booking form**. When you use the booking feature, we ask you to provide:

1. Your name or nickname;
2. An email address you use;
3. Your preferred contact method (Line, WhatsApp, Messenger, or phone) and the corresponding account or number;
4. The appointment date and time slot you select;
5. Optional notes (for example, a career or life challenge you're currently facing).

Beyond the Booking Information above, this website does not require you to register an account or sign in, and we do not separately ask for a password, national ID number, financial account information, or other sensitive personal data.

#### 2.2 Language Preference

Your language preference (Chinese / English) is stored only in your browser's local storage (localStorage). It is never sent to our servers and does not involve the collection of personal data.

## Chapter 2 · How We Process and Use Personal Data

### § 3 Purpose of Collection and Use

We collect the personal data described above solely to:

1. Confirm and arrange the 1-on-1 Coaching, Workshop, Small Group Class, or Keynote & Talk session you book;
2. Use the contact method you provide to confirm the timing, format, and other details of the session with you;
3. Communicate with you before and after a session, including reminders, change notices, and follow-up;
4. Keep any records legally required of us.

We do not use your personal data for marketing outreach, sale, rental, or disclosure to unrelated third parties beyond the purposes above, and we do not collect or use your data for advertising or cross-site tracking.

### § 4 Storage and Third-Party Service Providers

#### 4.1 Database Service

Information you submit through the booking form is transmitted through this website's backend and stored in a cloud database provided by **Supabase**, our database and backend service provider. In this capacity, Supabase acts as our data processor and handles the data under its own security measures and privacy policy.

#### 4.2 Email Delivery Service

We may use an email delivery service to send booking confirmations, change notices, or other service-related messages to the email address you provide.

These service providers may process your personal data only as needed to provide the Services on our instruction, and may not use it for their own marketing purposes.

### § 5 Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

This website does not include any usage-analytics tool, advertising tracker, cross-site tracking tool, or third-party advertising SDK.

We do not sell, rent, exchange, or otherwise provide your personal data to advertisers or data brokers.

If we add usage statistics, error reporting, or other tracking-related features in the future, we will update this Policy before enabling them and provide appropriate notice based on the nature of the feature.

### § 6 Data Retention

Your booking information is retained for a reasonable period after the corresponding session is completed, to support follow-up communication, dispute handling, and legal compliance. Once that period has passed and retention is no longer necessary, we will delete or anonymize the data within a reasonable time.

If you ask us to delete your booking information earlier, we will assist as described in § 9; however, transaction records from a completed session may need to be retained for a period required by applicable law.

## Chapter 3 · Data Security and Your Rights

### § 7 Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the personal data you provide through the booking form, including:

1. Using a cloud database service with access controls and encryption in transit;
2. Limiting access to booking information to authorized personnel, used only as needed to process bookings and communicate with you;
3. Periodically reviewing who has access to that data.

That said, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. We make reasonable efforts to protect your data, but cannot guarantee it will be entirely free from the risk of unauthorized access, use, or disclosure while in transit or storage.

### § 8 Your Rights Over Your Personal Data

Under Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act and other applicable law, you may exercise the following rights over the personal data we hold about you:

1. Request access to or a review of it;
2. Request a copy of it;
3. Request that it be supplemented or corrected;
4. Request that we stop collecting, processing, or using it;
5. Request that it be deleted.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in § 12. After verifying your identity, we will respond within a reasonable time; the exercise of these rights may still be subject to identity verification, legally mandated retention obligations, and other requirements of applicable law.

### § 9 Children and Minors

This website and the Services are not designed to collect personal data from children or minors.

If you are a minor, please obtain your legal guardian's consent before using the booking feature. If we become aware that we have collected a minor's personal data without such consent, we will delete that data as promptly as reasonably practicable.

## Chapter 4 · Other Provisions

### § 10 Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Policy due to changes in our services, technology, third-party providers, or legal requirements. The revised Policy will be posted on this website with an updated "last updated" date.

If a revision adds new categories of data collected, changes the purpose of collection, or otherwise could materially affect your rights, we will provide additional notice by reasonable means. Unless stated otherwise, the revised Policy takes effect from the date of posting.

This Policy and the Terms of Service should be read together. If they differ on matters of personal data handling, this Policy controls, except where applicable law mandates otherwise.
